Vibe Meter

A menu-bar usage meter for vibe coders.

See how much of your AI coding agents' limits you've used — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Antigravity — right from the macOS menu bar. Get a heads-up before you hit a limit, and pick the work back up once it resets.

  • Claude Code usage from its official status line, and Codex from read-only official rate-limit queries — 5-hour and weekly windows.
  • Low-usage notifications with an adjustable threshold, plus a copyable hand-off prompt so another agent can take over.
  • Resume flow for Codex sessions stopped by a usage limit, with strict checks — and a "needs confirmation" state when the data can't prove it's safe.
  • Only shows numbers it can trace to a real source; it never guesses usage.
  • English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese · macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon.
  • Swift
  • macOS
  • Open source

Notch Island

Turn the MacBook notch into a tiny control center.

Music controls, timers, a Pomodoro, and up to four widgets — battery, weather, stocks, calendar — living in the notch. Plus clipboard history, quick screenshots, and song recognition. In English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese.

  • Click the notch (or hover for a second) to open it; it stays out of the way of menu-bar space when idle.
  • Now playing from Apple Music, Spotify and optional YouTube / YouTube Music, with playback and scrubbing controls.
  • Up to four widgets — battery, weather, stocks, clock, calendar, timer, stopwatch and Pomodoro.
  • Clipboard history, screenshot-to-clipboard, screen recording and song recognition (asks for consent before any audio leaves the Mac).
  • Manual update check that verifies the download's SHA-256 against the GitHub release.
  • Swift
  • macOS
  • Open source

  • Timed keep-awake modes from the menu bar.

  • An advanced lid-closed option (still being verified on real hardware).
